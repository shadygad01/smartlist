MISSION
────────────────────────────────────────

The purpose of this platform is to discover statistically significant bottoming opportunities originating from Discount Areas within Egyptian equities and continuously improve its ability to identify those opportunities through autonomous research, validation, optimization, deployment, monitoring, and learning.

The platform is not a short-term trading system.

The platform is not a return forecasting engine.

The platform is not designed around fixed holding periods.

The platform is a self-improving bottom discovery and reversal research platform.
